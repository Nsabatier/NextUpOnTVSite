FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Where are my shows and progress stored?

Your library and progress are stored on your iPhone. No behavioural data is collected.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Next Up On TV without creating an account. Connections to Trakt and BetaSeries are entirely optional.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Once your library has been synced, you can browse it and track your progress without a network connection.

How do I import my library?

Next Up On TV can import your library and watch history from Trakt or BetaSeries through the app’s settings.

Which devices does the app support?

Next Up On TV is designed for iPhone and available in English and French.
