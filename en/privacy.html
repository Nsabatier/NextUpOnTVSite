PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Next Up On TV is designed to work without an account, advertising or behavioural tracking.

LAST UPDATED · 30 JULY 2026

Publisher

Next Up On TV is developed by Nicolas Sabatier, an independent developer. For any questions, email nextupontv@gmail.com.

What the app does

Next Up On TV is a TV show tracking app. It lets you manage your library, progress and upcoming releases directly on your iPhone. No account is required. You may optionally connect a Trakt or BetaSeries account to import or sync your data.

Data processed

Next Up On TV does not operate any server of its own to collect or store your personal data.

Library, progress and preferences

Storage: locally on your device.

Purpose: to operate the app, including offline.

Account identifiers and authentication tokens

Source: Trakt or BetaSeries, only when you connect one of these services.

Storage: the secure iOS Keychain.

Purpose: to connect your account and perform the sync you requested.

Purchase status

Source and storage: managed by Apple through StoreKit when a purchase is offered.

Purpose: to unlock the relevant features. Next Up On TV does not receive or store any payment card details.

Third-party services and network communication

Depending on the features you choose to use, the app may communicate with Trakt, BetaSeries and Apple. Connections to Trakt and BetaSeries are optional. Data exchanged with these services is governed by their own privacy policies: Trakt and BetaSeries.

On-device translation

Translations offered by the app are performed directly on your device whenever the feature is available. Your library content is not sent to an advertising or analytics service.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising tracking or profiling tools.
  • No collection of data for advertising purposes.
  • No sale of your personal data.
  • No personal data stored on a server belonging to Next Up On TV.

Notifications

App notifications are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No remote push notification service is used.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because Next Up On TV does not retain personal data on a server of its own, the publisher holds no data for you to access, correct or delete. You can manage your data directly in the app and remove all locally stored data by deleting the app from your iPhone.

If you connect Trakt or BetaSeries, you can disconnect the service from the app’s settings. Data held by those services remains managed through your account with the relevant service.

Children

Next Up On TV is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16. Optional use of Trakt or BetaSeries is subject to the terms of the relevant service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be mentioned in the release notes.

Contact us

For any questions about your data or this policy, email nextupontv@gmail.com or visit the support page.
